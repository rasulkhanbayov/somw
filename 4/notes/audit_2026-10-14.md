## Hard-reviewer objections, by severity

| # | Objection | Severity | Concrete fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "In distribution a coarse simulator alone is cheap (0.12×) and accurate (risk 0.046). Why generate at all?" | **high** | (a) Confirm on fresh shifted queries, pre-registered, that coarse-only fails silently under shift while the certified pipelines hold. (b) Add the obvious stronger baseline, a **cross-fidelity agreement cascade** (accept when the generation and the coarse rollout agree), and report it honestly whether it wins or loses. (c) Frame the generator as the cheap proposal a video pipeline already has. |
| 2 | "The shift results are exploratory" | **high** | Same fresh shifted queries; pre-registered tests per split. |
| 3 | "Adaptive compute rarely beats uniform at matched cost" (a Sep 2026 compute-value audit) | **high** | Report the uniform-window certificate at matched probe budget next to `spread_event` on fresh data. If they tie, say so: then the contribution is the certificate and the timing of repair, not where the windows go. |
| 4 | "Single object, elastic only" | medium | Multi-object contact benchmark (item 3); state the elastic scope as a deliberate choice (positions are a full state only for history-free materials). |
| 5 | "Base model only" | medium, but answerable | Phase 1/4d: the large model costs 4.52 s per sample against a 4.67 s reference rollout, so for it sparse probing cannot save compute. Present this as **when the method pays off**: the generator must be much cheaper than the simulator. Its seed-spread certificate is valid (FAR 0.103). |
| 6 | "Under hard shift the cost approaches always-simulate" | medium | This is the correct behaviour of a certified system (it notices the shift). Report the cost per split and the break-even condition. |
| 7 | "Restart timing has only a small effect (+0.002)" | low–medium | Report it as small but significant; the contact benchmark is where timing should matter (errors are local). |
| 8 | "Generative repair failed" | low, if reported well | A clear negative result with a diagnosis ("windows certify consistency, not accuracy"); the Goodhart finding (4c) is itself useful. |
| 9 | "No videos" (it is a video-generation venue) | **high for CVPR** | Render qualitative videos with PhysCtrl's trajectory-conditioned renderer (H200): generation vs certified-repaired vs ground truth. |
| 10 | "Amortisation promised but not shown" | medium | A learned probe policy or online conformal on the query stream (CPU, from existing scans); otherwise drop the claim from the abstract (needs the user's approval). |

## What decides acceptance

1. Objections 1–3 settled with **pre-registered fresh-query evidence**.
2. Videos.
3. Contact scenes.

Amortisation and external benchmarks come after these.
