What we want: low risk at a fraction of the simulation cost

The target is the bottom-left corner. Left: Phase 4k, pre-registered, 800 fresh queries. Right: Phase 2c, 300 queries incl. 200 shifted, exploratory.

The three goals, in order: (1) accept only what is right, with a calibrated guarantee: risk at or below the target 0.10, achieved; (2) pay much less than always simulating, achieved (0.53× in distribution) but not under hard shift, where nearly everything is rejected; (3) beat every cheap shortcut once the scene leaves the training distribution, shown on the right panel. The shortcuts look fine on the left and fail on the right.
