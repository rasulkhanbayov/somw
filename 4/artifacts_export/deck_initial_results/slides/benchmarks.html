Baselines already run: always generate, always simulate, coarse simulation only, certify or simulate, random fallback at the same rate, the model's seed spread as a certificate, shuffled restart times. Still to run from the proposal: uniform and random windows at a matched budget across all splits, ANCHOR-style threshold gating, best-of-N, oracle placement, and video-level comparisons (PhysFlow, RealWonder, 3DPhysVideo) with a VLM judge. The next experiment (multi-object contact) follows RigidBench's approach of measuring 3D error against a simulated reference, in our own simulator. The proposal also listed a real multi-object collision dataset (gs_physics), but it could not be verified, so it is left out.
