The repair keeps the generated frames up to the first failing window and re-simulates the rest, starting from a cheap coarse simulation's full state at that point. Acceptance uses the certificate's own 3 windows, so accepting costs the same as in certify-or-simulate. It was designed on earlier queries and then tested on 800 queries that no analysis had touched. It also stays cheaper at a much looser threshold (saving 0.26 s per query).
