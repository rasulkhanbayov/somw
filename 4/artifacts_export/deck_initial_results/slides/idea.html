Generate once, simulate only where it is likely wrong

1 · Generate

PhysCtrl's diffusion model outputs a 3D point trajectory (2,048 points, 24 frames)

2 · Probe

Short exact-simulator windows restart from states lifted out of the trajectory

3 · Certify

A conformal threshold on the window defect accepts or rejects the trajectory

4 · Repair

If rejected: keep the certified start, re-simulate the rest from a cheap coarse state

Claim: at matched compute, this beats both always-generate and always-simulate, and it certifies what it accepts.

Defect = mean distance between the generated frames and the simulator's window from the lifted state. No PDE residual is needed. The repair in step 4 is the "gated V2" variant, the one that survived testing (slides 6–8).
